This is the host-sim table: the same match loop every difficulty number was measured on, with you at seat 0. When a bid is challenged the two of them can double the stake back and forth (counter, escalate) before the cups come up, with no ceiling but the AI's own nerve; the loser pays the stake that stood, in full. Those rungs are yours when you are in the duel: stand, escalate, or (as the challenger) fold for the reduced penalty. So is a chain-kill sweep (challenge a run of recent bidders at once, at a depth you pick; each swept seat answers on its own bid, and you answer them), and the pause after a rival's bid, where you can call the count exact (a hit heals a die or sobers a drink, a miss costs one) or, on the ship's rule, challenge the bid out of turn. Every decision that costs you is yours. Ones are wild under any other face (飞 in the bar); a bid of ones makes them count as ones (斋 in the bar, aces on the ship, where they cannot open).
