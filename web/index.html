      How the table works
      
    
        
  • Bar - lives. Each seat has a set number of lives, shown on the seat. Lose a hand and you drink the stake: one life, doubled for each rung of a duel. At zero you are out. Calling a count exact gives a life back.
    • 
        
  • Ship - dice. Lose a hand, lose a die. Out of dice, out of the match.
    • 
        
  • Duels. A challenge can be doubled back and forth before the cups come up, to a hard stop at 32x; the loser pays the stake that stood. When the duel is yours: stand, escalate, or - as the challenger - fold for the reduced penalty.
    • 
        
  • Sweep. Challenge a run of recent bidders at once, at a depth you pick. Each swept seat answers on its own bid, and you answer them.
    • 
        
  • The beat after a rival's bid. Exact! - and, under the out-of-turn rule, Challenge - light up for one beat. Say nothing and it passes, as at a real table.
    • 
        
  • Bar bids are 飞 or 斋. 飞 ones are wild, 斋 ones count only as ones. A bid of ones is always 斋, any other face can be bid either way, and switching mid-round is priced. On the ship ones are wild, and a bid of aces counts aces alone and cannot open.
    •
